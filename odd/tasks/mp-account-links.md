# mp-account-links

Objective: split the Mercado Pago connection into an account and a channel link, so a store can change accounts (and accounts can move between stores) without losing refunds, late payments or chargebacks.
Branch: feat/mp-account-links · Delivery: ask-on-risk, work-unit commits on the branch (push/PR/merge are the user's call) · Runner: Vitest (SQL.js suites + PostgreSQL suites with `TEST_DB_PASSWORD`; throwaway container `tiendasaas-test-pg` on port 5433, user `postgres`, password `ci-password`).

## Specs

- S1 (checks before starting): `Order.code` is unique server-wide (confirmed: `@Index({ unique: true })` and `CREATE UNIQUE INDEX ... ON "order" ("code")`). The payment response carries `collector_id` (top level, number), but "that it matches the OAuth `user_id`" is NOT documented: the user decided to continue with a safe rejection (S9) and to verify in sandbox (S18).
- S2 (entities): "**`MercadoPagoAccount`**: una fila por cuenta de Mercado Pago. Tiene `mpUserId` (único), los tokens cifrados, `publicKey`, `expiresAt`, `scope` y el estado (`connected`, `disconnected`, `released`). Los tokens viven solo acá, una vez por cuenta." / "**`MercadoPagoChannelLink`**: el vínculo entre un local y una cuenta. Tiene `channelId`, la cuenta, el estado (`active`, `retired`, `released`), `linkedAt`, `retiredAt` y `releasedAt`." Rules enforced with partial unique indexes in the database: "Un local tiene **como máximo un vínculo `active`**." / "Una cuenta está **`active` en un solo local** a la vez." / "Los vínculos `retired` y `released` **no bloquean** nada." AES AAD is bound to the account, not the channel.
- S3 (migration): "cada conexión existente pasa a ser una cuenta y un vínculo `active`, conservando los tokens. Probala sobre una base con datos de la versión actual."
- S4 (payment metadata): each payment stores in `metadata` `mpAccountUserId` ("el `mpUserId` de la cuenta con cuyo token se consultó el pago. Es el que se usa para reembolsos y cancelaciones") and `mpCollectorId` ("el `collector_id` tal como lo devuelve Mercado Pago"). The migration fills existing payments with their channel's active account.
- S5 (webhook, mode `application`): find account by notification `user_id` (ignore + log + notify platform if missing or without tokens); query the payment with that account's token; find the order by `external_reference` across the whole server and take its channel; require that channel has or had a link (`active` or `retired`) with that account, else `ignored` and logged; all current validations stay (status, reference, currency, exact amount, claims table, `pg_advisory_xact_lock` per order, idempotency).
- S6 (mode `preference`): the route carries the channel token; try the accounts of the channel's `active` and `retired` links; the order must belong to the route's channel; "una notificación cuyo pedido es de otro canal que el de la ruta se ignora".
- S7 (`syncMercadoPagoPayment`): candidate accounts are those of the `active` and `retired` links of the order's channel.
- S8 (released link with an approved payment): with tokens still available the payment is processed normally and the platform is notified (the release rule failed); without tokens it cannot be verified: notify the platform, never discard silently.
- S9 (collector check, decision of L2): collector present and equal to the queried account's `mpUserId` → continue with the normal validations; "`collector_id` distinto o ausente: **no se liquida**. El reclamo queda en `needs_review` con el motivo `collector_mismatch` (o `collector_missing`) y se envía el aviso de atención al local y a la plataforma. Nunca se acepta en silencio." Applies to webhook, `syncMercadoPagoPayment` and the handler's `createPayment`. "No agregues una variable para desactivarla."
- S10 (refunds/cancellations): `createRefund` uses the token of the account stored in the payment `metadata`; `cancelMercadoPagoPayment` tries the accounts of the `active` and `retired` links of the channel and checks the payment belongs to an order of that channel; if the account is `released` or `disconnected` the refund is `Failed` with `metadata.errorMessage`: "Este pago se cobró en la cuenta <identificador>, que ya no está vinculada. Hacé el reembolso desde el panel de Mercado Pago de esa cuenta."
- S11 (OAuth callback, user_id X for channel C): X `active` in another channel → rejected, nothing changes; C already has X `active` → reconnection, tokens updated; C has another account Y `active` → C–Y becomes `retired` (`retiredAt` = now) and C–X is `active`; C has a `retired` link with X → that link is reactivated, no new one; X has `retired` links in other channels → allowed. The new tokens always replace the account's. "**Nunca se copian tokens de un local a otro.**"
- S12 (unlink/revocation): "Desvincular" turns the `active` link into `retired` and deletes nothing. Revocation (`mp-connect` or `GET /users/me` check) sets the account `disconnected`, which affects all its links. The `mercado-pago-connected` checker is eligible only if the channel has an `active` link with a `connected` account and a valid token. `createMercadoPagoCheckout` always uses the account of the `active` link.
- S13 (renewal): per account, serialised per account; renews every account with at least one `active` or `retired` link; adjust `npm run refresh-connections`.
- S14 (automatic release): daily scheduled task (default scheduler) moves a `retired` link to `released` when BOTH hold: 180 days since the last settled payment of that link (same channel and same account) and 30 days since `retiredAt`. Both windows configurable in the plugin (defaults 180 and 30). On release: record `releasedAt`; if the account has no `active` or `retired` links left, delete its tokens and leave it `released`; send an informational `MercadoPagoAttentionEvent` to the channel administrators. Plus an equivalent on-demand script in the style of `refresh-connections`.
- S15 (superadmin Admin API): "Retirar la cuenta de un local" (`active` → `retired`); "Liberar un vínculo ya": explicit confirmation in the input and, beforehand, it returns how many payments of that link could still be refunded.
- S16 (dashboard, Ajustes › Mercado Pago): show the active account and a list of previous accounts with their status and the estimated release date; before "Vincular otra cuenta" and before "Desvincular" show: "No revoques el acceso de esta cuenta desde Mercado Pago durante 180 días. Si lo hacés, los reembolsos de las ventas cobradas en ella vas a tener que hacerlos desde el panel de Mercado Pago." No operation returns tokens.
- S17 (mandatory tests): (1) change X→Y in a store: refund of an X payment works; a pending X payment approved after the change settles its order; a preference created with X and paid after the change settles its order; new preferences use Y. (2) transfer: X `retired` in A then linked in B: refund of an A sale works, a chargeback of A is assigned to A, B's payments go to B. (3) linking X in B while `active` in A is rejected without changes. (4) relinking X in the same store where it was `retired` reactivates the link. (5) release: no release if either condition is missing; releases when both hold; deletes tokens only if the account has no other links; keeps them if X is still `active` elsewhere. (6) refund of a payment whose link is released: `Failed` with the message of S10. (7) approved payment for a `released` link: with tokens it settles and notifies the platform; without tokens it notifies the platform. (8) concurrency: two simultaneous renewals of an account shared by two channels do not spend the same `refresh_token`. (9) eligibility: only with an `active` link; a `disconnected` account makes all its links ineligible. (10) `preference` mode: a notification whose order is from another channel than the route's is ignored. (11) migration: a database with current-version data ends with correct accounts, links and `metadata`. (12) the partial unique indexes prevent two `active` links in a store and one account `active` in two stores, against PostgreSQL. Plus `collector_id` different and absent: order not settled, claim `needs_review`, alert emitted.
- S18 (docs): `docs/PRD.md` sections 9.2, 9.3, 9.4, 9.5, 9.7, 9.9, 12 and 18.2 ([Implementado] with reference; fix the "`disconnected` no bloquea" sentence in 9.2); `DECISIONS.md` new entries with reasons; `VERIFICATION.md` ONE new sandbox point, among the first to run, comparing three ids on one test payment of an OAuth-linked seller: the `user_id` of `/oauth/token`, the `user_id` of the notification (V1) and the `collector_id` of `GET /v1/payments/{id}` ("Si no coinciden, detenete y avisame antes de tocar la regla, porque hay que rediseñarla."), plus a second point: whether a new OAuth authorization of the same account invalidates the previous tokens; `README.md`: how to change account, how to transfer an account between stores, what the superadmin tools do.
- S19 (rules): all non-negotiable rules of PRD section 11 stay. Nothing outside this scope: "cómo reaccionar a un 401 de Mercado Pago en el checkout: no lo implementes ahora." Stop and ask if a product decision is uncovered. Done = `npm run verify` green, the PostgreSQL suite run, and a summary of what changed and what is pending in sandbox.
